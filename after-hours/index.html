After Hours

After Hours is where the brief ends. When I’m not designing products, I’m making music, taking photos, sewing garments, sketching ideas, or filming moments that would otherwise disappear. This isn’t another portfolio, it’s a living archive of the work that exists outside deadlines, clients, and expectations. Every piece here comes from the same creative instinct, just expressed through different mediums
